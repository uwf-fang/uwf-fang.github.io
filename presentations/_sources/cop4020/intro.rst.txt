Course Description
------------------
+ Description:

  Programming language theory and practice, including language design and
  implementation, theoretical foundations, language translation, and exposure
  to a variety of programming paradigms.

+ Keywords:

  Syntax and Semantics; Programming Paradigms; Language Translation; Compilers
  vs Interpreters; Type Systems; Object-Oriented Programming; Functional
  Programming; Context-free Grammars; Memory Management; Inheritance and
  Polymorphism

Course Objective
----------------
+ Describe fundamentals issues in the design and implementation of programming
  languages.
+ Describe programming language theory as it pertains to programming language
  translation.
+ Develop programs using the major programming paradigms (procedural, object-
  oriented, functional and logic-based).

Exams

  * 75 minutes

  * 150 minutes
  * during final exam week
  * University schedule

Assignments
-----------
+ **4 Programming projects**
+ **1 Group project** team of 4-5 students
+ Late submission policy:

  * Two days of grace period. Not accepted after that.
  * **Not for projects due on the last day of the class!**
  * Late penalty: 10% < 1 day, 30% < 2 days
  * Exceptions: medical emergency, family emergency, etc. that will affect many
    days of your work. Please contact me as soon as possible.
  * 🚫 Excuses caused by poor time management, unreasonable personal engagement,
    situations that only affect a few days, forget the deadline, will not be
    considered for fairness to other students who finish on time.
  * Plan ahead and start early!
