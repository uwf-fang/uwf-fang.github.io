Event Details

  • Date: Nov. 7, 2026
  • Location: Florida State University, Tallahassee, FL
  • Hosted by: Florida State University
  • Results
    • Two UWF teams participated in Division 2.
    • The top UWF team solved 8 problems, earned gold in the FSU Division 2 section, and placed fourth in the regional Division 2 standings.
    • Three teams solved 9 problems; UWF was the only team to solve 8, while every other team solved fewer than 8.
    • The second UWF team solved 3 problems.

Contest and problems

The Southeast Regional 2026 contest on Kattis has the contest scoreboard and problem set. Each problem link opens its original statement on Kattis.
