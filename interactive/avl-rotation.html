AVL Rotation Lab

Start with an LL, RR, LR, or RL example, or generate a larger AVL tree. Step through search, insertion, or deletion; inspect balance factors bottom-up and watch each single rotation preserve the inorder sequence. Double rotations take two separate steps.

Begin prepares an operation. Step advances a comparison, structural update, balance check, or rotation. Reset replays the same operation. Build AVL and Generate random AVL create complete starting trees.
