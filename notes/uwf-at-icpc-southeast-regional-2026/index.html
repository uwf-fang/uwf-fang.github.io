Congratulations to our UWF ICPC teams

Two UWF teams competed at Florida State University, and one placed first at the FSU site and fourth in the region in Division 2.

November 11, 2026

On Saturday, November 7, two teams from the University of West Florida competed in Division 2 at the ICPC Southeast Regional at Florida State University in Tallahassee. One of our teams solved eight problems, placing first in Division 2 at the FSU site and fourth in Division 2 across the Southeast region. Our other team solved three problems. Congratulations to both teams for representing UWF! UWF contest recap

Even if you’ve never competed, I think these problems are worth a look. Often the interesting part comes before you write any code. You have to figure out what the problem is asking, decide which details matter, and look for a small example that might break your first idea. That kind of problem solving and critical thinking comes up in technical interviews, especially when you’re asked to explain why you chose an approach and how you know it works.

If you’re curious, pick a problem whose description sounds interesting and see how far you get. A few minutes spent working through a small example can give you a feel for what our teams were doing on Saturday. I’ve put together explanations and Python solutions for some of the problems if you’d like to see how one approach works. Division 2 problems on Kattis · Selected solution notes
