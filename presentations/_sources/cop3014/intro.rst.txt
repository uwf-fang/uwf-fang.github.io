Course Description
------------------
+ Description: An introduction to advanced computational and problem solving
  techniques. Emphasis on the use of basic programming constructs to create
  correct, efficient algorithms. Secondary focus on the basic structure and
  decomposition of programs.

+ Keywords:

  General purpose programming; C++ programming language; Development
  environment and workflow; Algorithms; Pseudocode; Algorithm analysis;

Course Objective
----------------
+ Develop algorithmic solutions to problems, using basic data types, control
  constructs, and parameter passing.
+ Build program solutions that require simple search and sorting algorithms.
+ Follow a development process where algorithms are developed before
  implementation begins.
+ Design and implement solutions to problems involving mathematical or
  scientific principles.
+ Analyze solutions for correctness and efficiency.

Exams
-----
+ Two midterm exams

  * 75 minutes
  * **First class** meeting time in week 6 and 11

+ Comprehensive final exam

  * 150 minutes
  * during final exam week
  * University schedule

Assignments
-----------
+ Programming projects

  * around 3 weeks to finish
  * 4 projects and 1 optional project

+ Labs: 1 week to finish, **no grace period**
+ Late submission policy:

  * Two days of grace period. Not accepted after that.
  * **Not for last project!**
  * Late penalty: 10% < 1 day, 30% < 2 days
  * Exceptions: medical emergency, family emergency, etc. that will affect many
    days of your work. Please contact me as soon as possible.
  * 🚫 Excuses caused by poor time management, unreasonable personal engagement,
    situations that only affect a few days, forget the deadline, will not be
    considered for fairness to other students who finish on time.
  * Plan ahead and start early!
