Overview
========
A brute-force algorithm is a straightforward and exhaustive problem-solving
approach that systematically explores all possible solutions or combinations to
a problem. It does not rely on advanced strategies or optimizations and instead
relies on sheer computational power to find a solution.

.. container:: flex-container

  .. container:: half

    **Characteristics**

    + Exhaustive
    + Naive
    + Simple
    + Inefficient: often :math:`\Theta(n!)` or :math:`\Theta(2^n)`

  .. container:: half

    **Applications**

    + Baseline for comparison and benchmarking
    + Small search spaces
    + All final states are solutions

      * Find all permutations/combinations of a set
      * Find all paths in a graph
